Notes
- Chain-track centre spacing is different from chain-link pitch.
- Roller centre spacing is different from the clear gap between rollers.
- Four-way fork entry does not approve four conveying directions.
- An empty-pallet run does not approve all loaded conditions.
- Use the site's approved trial, guarding and isolation procedures.
  Do not reach into running equipment or bypass guards or interlocks.
- Keep the approved configuration and change-review requirements with
  the quotation, purchase specification and sample acceptance record.
