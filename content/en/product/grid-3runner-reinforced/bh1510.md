## A long platform for extended unit loads

The extra 100 mm over BH1410 is meaningful for long products whose end support would otherwise be marginal. Keeping the width at 1000 mm limits unnecessary pallet area and helps preserve predictable load clearance.

## Automated handling characteristics

Its three runners and visible bottom structure make support orientation easy to define during equipment design. Reinforcement can follow the long axis to control repeated transfers between conveyor, shuttle and rack.
