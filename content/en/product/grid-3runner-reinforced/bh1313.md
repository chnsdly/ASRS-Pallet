## Deep square structure for centered heavy loads

The large square deck is well suited to drums, bulk packs and carton arrays that spread in both directions. Compared with a rectangular footprint, it simplifies load centering and leaves similar edge allowance on all four sides.

## Automated handling characteristics

At 170 mm high, BH1313 uses section depth to support a stable shape across a broad plan area. Three runners still establish a clear travel direction for rack and conveyor support, even though the upper deck is square.
