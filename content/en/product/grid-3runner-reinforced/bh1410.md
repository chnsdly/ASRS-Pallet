## Extra deck length without excessive width

The 1.4:1 footprint follows elongated goods more closely than a square pallet and reduces unsupported product overhang at the ends. A 1000 mm width remains practical for controlled aisle and conveyor envelopes.

## Automated handling characteristics

The reinforced grid and continuous runners distribute the longer load toward defined transfer points. This format is useful where the automation path is length-oriented and the unit load maintains one consistent direction.
