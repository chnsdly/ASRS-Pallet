## A balanced industrial footprint

The 1200 × 1000 mm format provides more deck width than a Euro-size pallet while remaining compact enough for high-density storage. It suits mixed cartons, returnable containers and industrial unit loads that need a generous support area without moving to an oversized AS/RS position.

## Smooth transfer through the automated flow

Three runners follow the 1200 mm length, creating continuous bearing lines for rack beams, chain conveyors and shuttle pickup zones arranged in the same direction. The rectangular footprint also gives controls and sensors a clear, repeatable orientation at inbound profile checks and transfer stations.
