## Extra length for production-scale unit loads

The 1700 × 1200 mm deck supports panel packs, long machinery components and production assemblies that need a stable base from manufacturing cell to storage. It reduces end overhang and keeps the unit load aligned with a purpose-designed large-format position.

## Designed around a dedicated automated route

At this length, runner contact and reinforcement layout become central parts of the machine interface. BH1712 provides clear lengthwise bearing lines and a 155 mm profile for a predictable journey through profile check, conveyor transfer and rack storage.
