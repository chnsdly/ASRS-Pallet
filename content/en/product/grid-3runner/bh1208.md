## Dense storage with a proven modular size

At 1200 × 800 mm, BH1208 is the narrowest pallet in this family. It supports tighter lane and conveyor planning, makes efficient use of European modular packaging and keeps the unit load compact for high-throughput carton and tote operations.

## Longitudinal runners for continuous transfer

The three 1200 mm runners present clear bearing paths for chain conveyors and rack supports. Its narrower 800 mm width also reduces the travel envelope at transfer points, helping automated systems maintain density without sacrificing four-way handling around staging areas.
