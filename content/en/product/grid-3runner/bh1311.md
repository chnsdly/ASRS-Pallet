## Extra length where 1100 mm is not enough

BH1311 extends the load platform to 1300 mm while retaining a controlled 1100 mm width. It fits rectangular cartons, equipment assemblies and returnable containers that would otherwise overhang a square pallet, yet remains more compact than the 1400 mm and 1500 mm ranges.

## A clear path through rack and conveyor interfaces

Continuous runners along the 1300 mm axis support long bearing lines and a consistent transfer direction. The proportion also makes load orientation obvious to sensors and operators, useful when the AS/RS stores a dedicated family of rectangular unit loads.
