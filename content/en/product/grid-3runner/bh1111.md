## Balanced support on a square footprint

The equal 1100 mm sides provide an even platform for square cartons, drums and compact process loads. The footprint is widely familiar across Pacific-region material flows and works well where a project wants one repeatable square load envelope from inbound check to storage position.

## Predictable orientation inside the AS/RS

Although the deck is square, the continuous runners still establish a clear machine direction for rack and conveyor support. This combines flexible four-way forklift access with a stable automated interface and helps simplify slot dimensions when unit loads have similar length and width.
