The open grid deck combines a lighter structure with airflow, drainage and easy visual inspection. Three continuous runners create predictable contact with rack supports and common conveyor layouts, while four-way entry keeps manual handling flexible around the automated flow.

## Built around the equipment interface

The range covers compact regional footprints, mainstream 1200 mm formats and extended project sizes up to 1800 × 1200 mm. This lets the pallet footprint follow the actual unit load instead of forcing oversized goods onto a smaller platform. Steel tubes can be positioned through the deck and runners to shape stiffness around rack span, shuttle pickup and conveyor transfer points.

## Configuration options

- Deck and runner reinforcement layouts for defined support conditions
- Anti-slip plugs and identification positions where supported by the mold
- Custom color, molded logo, printing and sequential fleet marking
- Interface review for roller conveyor, chain conveyor, shuttle and stacker crane systems
