## More deck area without an elongated load envelope

The 1200 × 1200 mm square platform supports wide cartons, drums and bulk packages that benefit from a centered, evenly distributed footprint. Compared with rectangular formats, it adds width while keeping both axes identical for straightforward load-building rules.

## A stable square unit load for automation

The runner direction fixes the machine interface while the square deck gives robots and palletizers a generous target area. It is especially useful where the storage position accepts broader loads but the operation still wants one consistent 1200 mm planning module.
