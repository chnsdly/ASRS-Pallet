## Transverse support for a different machine interface

BH1412-W uses the same broad 1400 × 1200 mm load platform as the other BH1412 variants, but turns the three runners so their length follows the 1200 mm pallet width. This creates continuous bearing lines across the shorter axis and gives system designers a distinct option for rack rails, chain strands or shuttle forks arranged around that direction.

## A deeper 160 mm structure

The 160 mm profile provides a substantial base for the transverse runner geometry and its reinforcement paths. It is well suited to wide industrial loads whose automated route has already been designed around widthwise support, while four-way fork entry keeps staging and maintenance handling straightforward.
