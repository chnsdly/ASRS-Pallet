## Added width for bulky rectangular unit loads

BH1513 expands the 1500 mm platform to 1300 mm wide, giving bulky cartons, grouped containers and equipment packs more complete edge support. It sits between the slimmer BH1512 and near-square BH1514, making it a useful middle ground for custom load profiles.

## Stable orientation for automated handling

The 1500 mm runner direction establishes the machine axis while the broader deck reduces unsupported load edges. This proportion works well in dedicated AS/RS cells where large packages remain rectangular but need more lateral stability during acceleration and transfer.
