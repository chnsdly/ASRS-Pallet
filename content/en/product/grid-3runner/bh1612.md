## A long platform without unnecessary width

BH1612 stretches to 1600 mm while holding width at 1200 mm, matching long equipment cartons, assemblies and component packs more closely than a broader square pallet. This helps keep the automated load envelope focused around the actual goods.

## Continuous support along the extended axis

Lengthwise runners and configurable steel paths control the structure along its 1600 mm span. The underside view gives integrators a direct reference for rail spacing and transfer contacts when building a dedicated large-format AS/RS route.
