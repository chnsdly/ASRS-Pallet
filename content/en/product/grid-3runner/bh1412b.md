## Deeper body for a stronger large-format base

BH1412B retains the practical 1400 × 1200 mm load area but increases overall height to 160 mm. The deeper structure provides more room for rib geometry and reinforcement paths, making it the performance-focused lengthwise variant in the BH1412 group.

## Made for repeated rack and transfer cycles

The three runners extend along 1400 mm to maintain stable bearing through long conveyor and rack interfaces. This model suits large unit loads that value base stiffness and repeated automated handling more than the 10 mm of vertical clearance saved by BH1412A.
