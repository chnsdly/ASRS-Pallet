## Maximum length for oversized industrial goods

BH1812 provides the family’s longest continuous deck for large components, elongated containers and oversized production loads. The 1200 mm width controls the lateral envelope while the 1800 mm axis gives the goods the support length they need.

## A purpose-built AS/RS load carrier

The long runner paths and expanded reinforcement range support a dedicated automated handling route rather than a standard pallet position. Its proportions make the load direction unmistakable, helping profile checks, conveyors and storage equipment maintain one consistent orientation from entry to retrieval.
