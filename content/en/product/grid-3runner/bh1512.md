## Extra deck length for elongated loads

The 1500 × 1200 mm footprint supports long cartons, panel packs and industrial assemblies with less end overhang than mainstream 1200 or 1400 mm pallets. Its 1200 mm width remains familiar while the added length follows the geometry of the load.

## Lengthwise bearing through dedicated AS/RS positions

Three runners track the full 1500 mm direction, creating continuous support lines for a dedicated large-format rack or conveyor route. The 150 mm height keeps this extended platform relatively low and preserves useful vertical space for the unit load.
