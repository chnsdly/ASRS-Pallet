## Broad support in a 150 mm profile

BH1412A pairs a 1400 × 1200 mm deck with a familiar 150 mm overall height. The generous platform supports wide machinery cartons, layered components and large returnable packaging while keeping more of the storage position available for the goods themselves.

## Lengthwise stability for large unit loads

Its runners follow the 1400 mm axis, giving long continuous support under the load and a direct interface for similarly oriented rack rails or chain conveyors. The A variant is the natural BH1412 choice when vertical clearance is a priority.
