## Broad, near-square support for large packs

With only 100 mm difference between length and width, BH1514 offers a generous platform for bulk containers, large bagged loads and wide industrial packaging. The near-square deck spreads the load across a broad area while the runner direction still gives the AS/RS a fixed handling axis.

## A dedicated large-format machine interface

The 155 mm body and continuous 1500 mm runners form a substantial base for long dwell times and repeat transfers. A bottom view makes the bearing geometry easy to review against support rails, chain strands and shuttle contact positions during system design.
