## Best fit

This family starts from the solid three-runner concept and adds a higher reinforcement envelope for demanding unit loads, wider support spans and long service cycles. It is intended for projects where deflection management and repeatability are more important than minimum pallet weight.

## Configuration options

- Multiple steel-tube layouts through deck and runners
- Anti-slip pads for improved unit-load stability
- Custom color, marking, molded logo and identification features
- Pre-production interface and sample review for automated handling systems

## Engineering note

“Heavy duty” does not replace project verification. Final rack capacity must be confirmed against the exact support span, temperature, dwell time, load distribution and steel layout. Published loads support preliminary selection.
