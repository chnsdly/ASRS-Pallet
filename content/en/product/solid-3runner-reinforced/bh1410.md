## Long closed deck with controlled width

The rectangular plan gives elongated goods more end support without consuming the width of a 1200 or 1400 mm square pallet. It is especially effective for loads that travel lengthwise through a fixed lane.

## Automated handling characteristics

A defined long axis lets steel tubes and runners follow the primary bending direction. This produces a clear interface for chain conveyors, shuttle pickup and rack support under an extended closed deck.
