## Heavy-duty structure in a familiar footprint

This model fits mixed industrial cartons, bags and returnable packaging while avoiding an oversized deck. The solid top gives continuous contact and helps isolate automated equipment from loose product residue.

Expanded steel positions carry the load toward three continuous runners and defined rack supports. It is a strong starting point for projects that need a standard footprint but tighter control of repeated racking deflection.
