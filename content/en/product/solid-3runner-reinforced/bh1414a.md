## Very broad square deck in the lower profile

The equal-sided deck is intended for bulk packs, drums and large carton patterns whose width approaches their length. It leaves balanced edge support and avoids forcing a broad load onto a narrow platform.

## Automated handling characteristics

Because the plan area is large, steel placement and machine support alignment are central to the configuration. The A profile keeps storage height efficient while the runners establish a fixed travel axis.
