## Deeper support beneath a broad closed deck

The footprint keeps large cartons, bags and fixtures fully supported, while the deeper body supplies more space for the reinforced load path. It is the higher-section counterpart to BH1412A.

## Automated handling characteristics

The 170 mm height is valuable where a wide deck crosses defined conveyor and rack supports. Steel tubes can be arranged to control the broad span as the pallet repeatedly changes support conditions.
