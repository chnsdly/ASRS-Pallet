## Broad rectangular deck in a compact A profile

The 1100 mm width supports fuller packaging patterns than BH1410 while preserving a clearly rectangular shape. It suits fixtures and carton groups that are broad but still have one dominant direction.

## Automated handling characteristics

The lower A profile is useful where rack pitch or transfer clearance is tightly controlled. Reinforcement capacity remains focused along the 1400 mm span and its three runner support lines.
