## Maximum deck breadth with the deep B structure

The square deck supports goods that occupy substantial area in both directions, while the B profile gives the reinforced body additional vertical depth. It is selected when the BH1414 plan size is right but the load path needs the stronger section.

## Automated handling characteristics

Three runners define machine orientation beneath the symmetric deck, and the 170 mm section helps bridge transfers between different support patterns. This makes the model suitable for large automated unit loads with repeated rack cycles.
