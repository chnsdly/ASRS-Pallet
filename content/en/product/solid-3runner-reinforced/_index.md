This family combines a continuous closed deck with deeper ribs and an expanded reinforcement envelope. It is intended for large unit loads that need a clean surface and tighter deflection control as they move between conveyor, shuttle and rack support.

## Two structural depths for repeated footprints

Several footprints are offered in A and B versions. The 150 mm A structure keeps the interface compact, while the 160 or 170 mm B structure adds section depth for more demanding spans and concentrated loads. This lets projects keep the same plan dimensions while selecting the vertical structure that better suits the equipment route.

## Configuration options

- Expanded deck and runner steel layouts for broad support spans
- Anti-slip elements and load-location features
- Custom colors, logos, printing and serial identification
- Interface review for conveyors, shuttles, stacker cranes and racks
