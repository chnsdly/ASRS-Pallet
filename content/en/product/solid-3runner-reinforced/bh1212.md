## Reinforced square deck for balanced loading

Equal length and width make this model suitable for loads that spread in both directions and may rotate during upstream handling. The reinforced closed deck supports flexible or small-base packaging without grid openings.

## Automated handling characteristics

Below the square deck, three runners still establish a fixed support direction for the AS/RS. Steel capacity is concentrated around the broad deck and its transfer into rack support lines.
