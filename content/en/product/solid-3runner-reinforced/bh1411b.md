## Deep B profile for the 1400 × 1100 footprint

The deck supports wide elongated goods on a continuous surface, while the B structure addresses longer transitions and concentrated load zones. It is the stronger vertical alternative to BH1411A.

## Automated handling characteristics

At 170 mm high, the pallet develops more section depth between deck and runner base. This helps the reinforced load path remain stable as the unit moves from conveyor support to shuttle or rack beams.
