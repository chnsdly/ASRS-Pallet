## Same footprint, deeper B structure

Keeping the same plan dimensions lets a project upgrade vertical stiffness without redesigning the load footprint. The closed deck remains suitable for bags, cartons and loads with small contact feet.

## Automated handling characteristics

The extra 10 mm of height and broader steel range provide more section depth for rack and transfer spans. This version is useful when BH1211A fits the goods but the equipment path demands a stronger vertical structure.
