## Best fit

The closed deck is designed for applications that value easy cleaning, spill control and a continuous load surface. Integrated anti-slip pads improve carton and container stability, while the three-runner base supports rack beams and powered conveyor sections.

## Configuration options

- Anti-slip pads on the deck as standard for this family
- Optional deck and runner steel tubes for defined rack spans
- Custom colors, logo, printing and traceability marking
- Perimeter lips, drainage details or RFID zones where the selected mold permits

## Engineering note

Published loads support preliminary selection. Actual performance varies with mold, temperature, support condition, load distribution and reinforcement. The approved data sheet and test method take precedence for every order.
