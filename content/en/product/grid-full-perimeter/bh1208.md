## Euro-format deck with a continuous perimeter base

The familiar 1200 × 800 mm footprint fits modular cartons and compact automated lanes. Open deck cells reduce trapped debris and water, while the surrounding base improves support when the pallet orientation changes.

## Automated handling characteristics

This model is useful for systems that combine standardized Euro loads with turntables or cross transfers. The full-perimeter base keeps the lower interface available along both long and short sides.
