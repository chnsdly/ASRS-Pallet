## Industrial footprint for mixed-direction flows

The footprint accommodates mixed carton patterns and returnable containers without becoming square. Its grid surface supports airflow and fast inspection, making it a flexible base for general industrial loads.

## Automated handling characteristics

A continuous perimeter gives conveyors and transfer devices more freedom to contact the pallet from different directions. This is especially useful where a single unit load passes through several machine orientations.
