The open grid deck supports airflow, drainage and quick visual inspection. Below it, the continuous full-perimeter base spreads contact around all four sides, improving stacking alignment and creating more available contact edges for mixed conveyor and support layouts.

## Broad bottom contact for changing orientations

Unlike a three-runner pallet with one dominant travel direction, the full-perimeter base offers a more symmetrical lower interface. It is useful where pallets rotate, transfer between different conveyor directions or require positive perimeter contact when stacked. Six footprints cover compact, rectangular and broad square unit loads.

## Configuration options

- Optional steel reinforcement for rack and transfer spans
- Anti-slip plugs and identification positions
- Custom colors, logos, printing and fleet numbering
- Interface review for rotation tables, conveyors, shuttles and racks
