## Large ventilated deck with four-sided support

The generous plan area reduces product overhang for loads that need real width in both directions. Grid openings retain airflow and drainage across a surface that would otherwise form a large closed pocket.

## Automated handling characteristics

The continuous perimeter helps large pallets transfer between different machine directions without relying on only three runner lines. Steel reinforcement can be concentrated around the support pattern used by rack, shuttle and conveyor.
