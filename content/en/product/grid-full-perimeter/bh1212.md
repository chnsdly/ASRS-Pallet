## Direction-neutral square deck and base

Equal dimensions suit goods that spread evenly in both axes and make edge allowance easy to balance. The ventilated deck reduces closed pockets beneath broad loads.

## Automated handling characteristics

The square footprint and perimeter base are well matched to rotation tables and orthogonal transfers. The pallet can change direction while retaining comparable lower support geometry.
