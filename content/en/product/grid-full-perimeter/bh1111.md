## Square footprint with all-around base contact

Equal sides simplify centering for square loads and leave similar edge support in every direction. The open grid supports airflow and inspection, while the perimeter base improves alignment when pallets are stacked.

## Automated handling characteristics

The symmetric lower interface is useful where a pallet rotates or moves between conveyor directions. Support can be picked up along more edges than on a conventional three-runner base.
