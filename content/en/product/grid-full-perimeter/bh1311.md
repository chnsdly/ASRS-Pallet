## Intermediate size with broader perimeter support

The extra length and width reduce overhang for larger cartons and fixtures. It remains more compact than BH1412 and is therefore easier to place in projects with moderate aisle and slot envelopes.

## Automated handling characteristics

Full-perimeter contact supports direction changes across the enlarged footprint. Optional reinforcement can be aligned with rack spans and cross-transfer contact points.
