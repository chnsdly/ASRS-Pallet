## Mainstream industrial footprint with continuous support

The 1200 × 1000 mm platform balances usable deck area with a manageable machine envelope. It accommodates mixed carton patterns and returnable containers without the added width of a square pallet.

## Automated handling characteristics

A 160 mm structure gives the runner openings and deck ribs additional depth for repeated automated handling. This model is a practical baseline when conveyors, racks and inbound handling all need one common footprint.
