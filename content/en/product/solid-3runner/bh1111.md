## Balanced square support with a deeper profile

Equal sides make load centering straightforward and support goods that spread similarly in both directions. The solid deck is especially useful when small packages or residues should not pass through the pallet.

## Automated handling characteristics

The 165 mm height provides additional vertical section compared with the 150 mm formats. Although the footprint is square, the runners still tell conveyor and rack designers exactly which direction carries the continuous support.
