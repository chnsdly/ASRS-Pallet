## Compact closed deck for smaller load envelopes

This is the smallest solid-deck footprint in the family. It works well when the goods do not justify a 1200 mm platform and when keeping the pallet close to the load outline improves slot density.

## Automated handling characteristics

The 150 mm structure keeps fork and conveyor interfaces conventional while three runners provide a stable machine direction. With no grid openings, labels, small feet and flexible packaging receive continuous support.
