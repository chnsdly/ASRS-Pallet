## An intermediate footprint for wider industrial loads

The added length and width are useful for cartons, bags or fixtures that slightly overrun a 1200 × 1000 mm deck. It reduces overhang while keeping a more compact aisle envelope than the largest family model.

## Automated handling characteristics

The rectangular plan keeps a clear machine direction, and the solid deck prevents small parts or residues from reaching equipment below. Additional reinforcement positions support projects with wider transfer gaps.
