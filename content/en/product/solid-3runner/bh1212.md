## Broad square deck for evenly spread goods

The large square plan gives six modular zones or balanced bulk loads room to sit without favoring one deck direction. It is more suitable than a narrow rectangle when product width and length are comparable.

## Automated handling characteristics

Three runners retain a defined support axis beneath the direction-neutral deck. The 150 mm height keeps the pallet compact for storage while preserving four-way handling around automated cells.
