## Best fit

The open grid deck reduces material weight and supports airflow, drainage and easy visual inspection. Three continuous runners create stable contact on rack beams and common conveyor arrangements, while four-way entry keeps forklift handling flexible.

## Configuration options

- Steel tubes in the deck and/or runners to manage rack deflection
- Anti-slip plugs, edge lips and identification areas where the mold permits
- Custom color, molded logo, screen printing or sequential marking
- Sample and drawing confirmation for conveyor, shuttle and stacker-crane interfaces

## Engineering note

Published loads are temporary market-reference values, not a universal rating. Final capacity depends on temperature, load distribution, support span, steel-tube configuration and test method. Confirm every AS/RS application against the approved drawing and test agreement.
