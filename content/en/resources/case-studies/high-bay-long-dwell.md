For a high-bay warehouse storing boxed goods, the main risk was long-term deflection across a defined beam span. A reinforced solid three-runner platform was shortlisted for its continuous deck and expanded tube options.

The confirmation plan included load-distribution drawings, steel layout, maximum dwell time and a sample rack test. Conveyor transitions and sensor targets were checked before the pallet specification was frozen.

*This application scenario illustrates the selection method; final specifications require project review and validation.*
