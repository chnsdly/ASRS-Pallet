A grid three-runner pallet was considered for a cold-storage flow requiring drainage, visual inspection and four-way forklift access. The project review focused on low-temperature material behavior, rack span and anti-slip requirements.

A representative sample was conditioned to the operating temperature and tested across rack and conveyor interfaces before approval.

*This application scenario illustrates the selection method; final specifications require project review and validation.*
