A mixed conveyor line used rollers, chains and mechanical stops. The full-perimeter base offered a broader contact pattern, but runner positions and transfer clearances still required drawing review.

The team mapped every transition, confirmed entry direction and ran a loaded sample through the critical points before production.

*This application scenario illustrates the selection method; final specifications require project review and validation.*
