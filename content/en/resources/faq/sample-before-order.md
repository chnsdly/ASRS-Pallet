Yes. For AS/RS projects, sample confirmation is strongly recommended. Please provide interface drawings and test conditions so the sample represents the final application.

Use our [AS/RS pallet sample testing and acceptance checklist]({{< relref "resources/insights/asrs-pallet-sample-testing-checklist.md" >}}) to prepare the sample specification, inspection record and equipment trial plan before a bulk order.
