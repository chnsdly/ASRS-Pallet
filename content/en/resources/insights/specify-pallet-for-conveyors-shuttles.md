Automated handling depends on repeatable interfaces. Start with conveyor type, roller or chain pitch, pallet travel direction, transfer points and locator geometry.

## Check the bottom pattern

Runner positions must maintain stable contact throughout movement. Confirm that openings, ribs and steel tubes do not conflict with chains, rollers, stops or lifting elements.

## Check sensing and centering

Share sensor position, expected target surface and any centering mechanism. Color and surface geometry can affect optical detection.

## Approve by interface

Review a dimensional drawing, then run a sample through all critical transitions under a representative unit load. A successful static test alone does not prove conveyor compatibility.
