**For a repeat AS/RS pallet order, identify the previously approved family, model, configuration, drawing revision and applicable sample or approval record. Ask the supplier to compare the proposed supply with that reference, then have the responsible teams review any change or unknown that could affect the warehouse.** A familiar product name or matching footprint alone does not establish an unchanged specification.

This guide helps a buyer prepare a repeat-order decision. The [sample acceptance checklist]({{< relref "resources/insights/asrs-pallet-sample-testing-checklist.md" >}}) covers planning and documenting a sample evaluation; here the question is how to connect that approved result to a later purchase and what to do if something changes.

## 1. Find the exact reference used for approval {#baseline}

Collect the last agreed purchase specification, drawing and revision, supplier quotation or order confirmation, sample identification and the documented approval scope. Note the relevant handling route, support arrangement, load and environment associated with that decision. If several records disagree, ask the supplier and warehouse owner which version was actually approved before describing the reorder as unchanged.

Keep the **product family plus model and configuration**, not just the model code. For example, the current BAOHENG catalogue lists [BH1210 Grid 3-Runner]({{< relref "product/grid-3runner/bh1210.md" >}}) and [BH1210 Solid 3-Runner]({{< relref "product/solid-3runner/bh1210.md" >}}) with the same 1200 × 1000 mm footprint but listed heights of 150 mm and 160 mm respectively. These are different catalogue entries, not evidence of a batch change or interchangeability. Confirm the drawing and configuration for the actual order.

## 2. Ask for a comparison against the reference {#comparison}

Use one record for the approved reference and the proposed supply. Ask the supplier to mark each relevant item **unchanged, changed or to confirm**, with the source and revision. A blank field is not confirmation that nothing changed.

| Item to compare | Record from the approved reference | If different or unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and geometry | Family, model, drawing revision, overall dimensions and relevant tolerances | Request the proposed drawing and have the equipment owner review affected interfaces |
| Underside and pickup | Runner layout and contact areas, openings, orientation and critical clearance locations | Compare against conveyor, rack and pickup drawings; agree any further check |
| Material and reinforcement | Agreed material specification and, where applicable, reinforcement quantity, position and configuration | Ask the supplier to explain the proposed configuration and provide relevant supporting information |
| Colour, surface and identification | Agreed colour reference, sensing surface, markings and label position where relevant | Ask the system owner whether detection or reading checks are affected |
| Load and environment | Approved load distribution, support, temperature and storage duration, with the applicable evidence | Compare the evidence with intended use; do not transfer a capacity figure to a changed condition |
| Supply and receipt | Quantity, packing, markings, documents and agreed receipt checks | Agree the applicable order terms and inspection responsibilities |

These fields are a purchasing aid, not a claim that every change makes a pallet unsuitable. The [rack-load evidence guide]({{< relref "resources/insights/rack-load-is-not-a-single-number.md" >}}) explains why a rating must remain connected to its support and load conditions.

## 3. Decide what needs review before release {#change-review}

If the agreed configuration and operating conditions are confirmed unchanged, the buyer and supplier can refer to the existing approval and apply the receipt checks agreed for the order. If a proposed change or missing information may affect an approved interface or the evidence behind the decision, refer it to the relevant owner **before accepting it as equivalent**. A colour change, for example, may affect some optical sensing arrangements; [OMRON explains](https://www.ia.omron.com/support/guide/43/introduction.html) that a diffuse-reflective sensor's performance varies with the target surface. Other sensor types and installations behave differently. Use the actual [sensor validation guide]({{< relref "resources/insights/asrs-pallet-color-sensor-detection.md" >}}) where relevant.

Agree whether the evidence already covers the proposal or whether a drawing review, representative sample or additional route check is needed. The equipment provider and project owner define the scope and criteria; do not copy another project's sample count, dimensional tolerance or load limit. A previously passed empty-pallet check does not by itself cover a changed loaded route.

[Dematic's supplier quality manual, section 5](https://www.dematic.cn/content/dam/dematic/downloads/policies/qa/Dematic_Supplier_Quality_Manual.pdf), is an example of a particular company's process for reviewing supplier changes after an applicable approval. Its requirements apply to its own suppliers and agreements; they are not a universal pallet certification rule or evidence of a BAOHENG approval process.

## 4. Put the agreed result on the purchase record {#purchase-record}

The purchasing team and supplier should identify which drawing and configuration the order refers to, the agreed quantity, and the approval reference. If a change was accepted, attach its description, applicable evidence, decision maker and effective order or batch. If a point remains unresolved, mark it **to confirm** and assign a next step instead of treating it as approved.

At receipt, record the product identity and batch information that the parties agreed to provide; compare the relevant features with the accepted reference using the agreed method. A receipt check cannot prove an unmeasured long-term rack performance. If an inconsistency is found, hold the affected pallets under the site's procedure and ask the responsible parties to assess it. The [pallet inspection guide]({{< relref "resources/insights/asrs-plastic-pallet-inspection-checklist.md" >}}) helps document the condition and disposition.

## A practical repeat-order message {#enquiry}

You can send the supplier a request such as: “Please quote [quantity] against [family and model], configuration [reference], drawing [number and revision] and approved sample or order [reference]. Identify any proposed difference in material, reinforcement, dimensions, colour or markings, and any item still to confirm. Please include the applicable packing, lead time and receipt documents in your reply.” Replace the brackets with actual project details; availability, terms and documentation are confirmed for each order.

[Contact BAOHENG]({{< relref "contact.md" >}}) with the reference and any change in load, equipment or environment. We can discuss the candidate configuration, available drawings and sample arrangements; equipment compatibility and project release remain with the responsible reviewers.
