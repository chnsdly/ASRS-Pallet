**For a load carried on feet, drum rims or other limited contact areas, record where the complete load touches the pallet and ask the supplier and equipment owner to review that exact arrangement.** A catalogue rating for a different load distribution or support cannot by itself approve the proposed use. The rack supports, load restraint, temperature and time also matter.

The [rack-load comparison guide]({{< relref "resources/insights/rack-load-is-not-a-single-number.md" >}}) explains how to read a rating and test report. This guide focuses on the separate purchasing task of describing a non-uniform load and reviewing a proposed load-spreading layer.

## 1. Describe the actual contact {#contact}

Photograph the underside of the goods and draw a plan view using a marked pallet reference side. Record the number, shape, dimensions and positions of feet, rails, drum rims or other contact areas; distinguish measured contact from an estimate. Identify protrusions, sharp edges, any overhang and the direction in which the unit travels. A drum rim, a container on feet and a carton across the deck do not necessarily apply their mass in the same places.

Record the total payload mass and its maximum operating condition, including contents and packaging. Give the complete moving mass separately, including the pallet and any intermediate layer. Mark the centre of gravity and any expected shift; do not assume that four feet each carry one quarter of the weight unless the responsible engineer has established that distribution.

[Virginia Tech's Center for Packaging and Unit Load Design](https://www.unitload.vt.edu/education/white-papers/5-wp-load-carrying-capacity-of-pallets.html) distinguishes point, line and other load patterns and stresses specifying both loading and support. It does not provide a rating for a particular BAOHENG configuration.

## 2. Match the contact to every support condition {#support}

| Review point | Information to share |
| --- | --- |
| Pallet | Family, model, drawing revision, deck, underside, material and any reinforcement configuration. |
| Load contact | Contact sketch and dimensions, payload, offset and expected changes in contents or position. |
| Rack | Support direction, location, contact width and clear span; the allowed positioning range comes from the system provider. |
| Handling | Fork or shuttle contact and movement, conveyor transfers, full unit dimensions and total moving mass. |
| Environment | Temperature range, longest loaded storage duration and relevant cleaning or exposure. |

Ask the supplier which evidence covers the same pallet configuration, loading pattern and supports. If a report uses a uniformly distributed load, request an assessment of the actual contact rather than applying an invented reduction factor. A flat floor, two beams and a shuttle platform are different support conditions. Use the [unit-load dimensions guide]({{< relref "resources/insights/asrs-pallet-unit-load-dimensions.md" >}}) to document the finished unit.

## 3. Consider a spreading layer as a new configuration {#layer}

A rigid board, plate or frame may be proposed to change how the load reaches the pallet. Its effectiveness depends on its stiffness, geometry, contact and how it is supported. A layer does not automatically spread the load evenly or make a pallet acceptable. Ask the goods owner, pallet supplier and system provider to review the whole assembly, including the load on the layer and the layer on the deck.

Record the layer's dimensions, mass, material, orientation and attachment or restraint, plus its contact on both sides. Include its effect on unit height, total mass, centre of gravity, sensors, pickup, friction and storage requirements. If it shifts, overhangs, covers a required interface or has insufficient evidence, keep the proposal pending until the responsible parties agree a suitable assessment. Do not select a thickness or claim a capacity increase from this article.

## 4. Compare the evidence and agree the trial {#evidence}

For each offer, keep the test or assessment reference and identify the pallet configuration, payload pattern, support geometry, temperature, duration, observations and any exclusions. Separate a supplier declaration from an applicable test or engineering assessment. A higher number without matching conditions does not resolve the comparison.

For a representative sample, the project owner and equipment provider should agree the actual load or an appropriate representation, contact positions, route, storage support, environmental conditions, observations and acceptance criteria in advance. Record the sample identity, drawings and results. Do not improvise an overload test or enter running machinery; follow the site's approved isolation and trial procedures. A short trial alone does not establish long-term rack performance. See the [sample acceptance checklist]({{< relref "resources/insights/asrs-pallet-sample-testing-checklist.md" >}}).

## Prepare an enquiry {#enquiry}

[Contact BAOHENG]({{< relref "contact.md" >}}) with a load-bottom sketch and photographs, maximum payload and complete mass, pallet orientation, rack support drawing, temperature and storage duration. Identify any proposed spreading layer and the quantity required.
